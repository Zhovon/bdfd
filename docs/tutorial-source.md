# Food Officers' Portal — Tutorial Source

> Source material for a NotebookLM tutorial. It describes what the portal is and
> the exact steps members and administrators follow. Written to be turned into a
> narrated video walkthrough.

## What the portal is

A private, members-only online communication platform for food officers and
employees working at establishments across the country. It brings colleagues
together across postings with notices, tour planning, mutual welfare, and a
blood-donor directory. Access is restricted to verified officers and employees;
a new account works only after an administrator approves it.

The portal is organised into five boards (modules):

- **Association Information** — about the association and committee, official notices, neutral election information.
- **Travel & Tourism** — tour programmes and plans, shared photos, and a "next trip" poll. This is the only board where a notice can collect a payment.
- **Welfare** — support notices for colleagues in need.
- **Condolence & Support** — remembering colleagues and standing by their families.
- **Blood Directory** — volunteer donor officers, searchable by blood group.

There are three roles: **member**, **moderator**, and **admin**. Moderators and
admins are "staff" and can post and manage content; admins additionally manage
members and settings.

## Part 1 — For members (end users)

### Registering and getting approved
1. Open the site and choose **Register**.
2. Fill in your details (name, official email, password) and complete the anti-spam check.
3. Submit. Your account is now **pending** — an administrator must approve it.
4. When approved, you receive an email and can log in. Until then, the members' area is locked.

### Logging in
1. Choose **Log in**, enter your official email and password, pass the security check.
2. You land on the **members' dashboard**.

### The dashboard
- A personal greeting and your role.
- Stat tiles: number of **Tours**, **Blood donors** on call, and **Notices** posted.
- **Latest posts** shown as cards, and an **older notices** strip.
- A left-hand menu links to every board, your notifications, and your profile.

### Reading a notice
1. Open any board (e.g. Travel & Tourism) and click a notice card.
2. A notice has a title, summary, a **photo gallery** (click to open a full-screen lightbox with arrow-key navigation), optional **videos**, and body text broken into **sections** (for a tour: Day 1, Day 2, Day 3), each of which can carry its own inline photos.
3. If a **programme PDF** is attached, it appears in an embedded viewer with a download button.

### Long lists are paginated
Boards show a page of notices at a time with page controls at the bottom, so
even with a lot of content the pages stay fast and tidy.

### Paying for a tour
Only Travel & Tourism notices can collect a payment. A notice may be:
- **Participation** — a fixed per-person fee, or
- **Donation** — an open amount the payer chooses.

To pay:
1. On the notice, use the payment button (e.g. "Participate · ৳3,000").
2. Choose a payment method (bank or mobile financial service) and follow the on-screen account details and instructions.
3. Send the money, then enter your **transaction reference** and submit.
4. An administrator verifies it; you get a notification confirming (or querying) your payment.

### Blood directory
Open **Blood Directory** and filter volunteer donors by blood group to find a
match quickly.

### Polls
On the Travel board you can vote in the current "next trip" poll and see the
results of past polls.

### Notifications and profile
- The **bell** shows unread notifications (account approval, new notices, payment updates). Opening the list marks them read.
- **My profile** lets you update your details and avatar.

## Part 2 — For administrators and staff

Staff reach the tools from the **Admin** panel.

### Creating a notice (the core task)
1. Go to **Admin → Content** and use the post editor.
2. Choose the **board**, write a **title** and a short **summary** (the summary shows on the card).
3. Add **cover photos** — the first one becomes the card's main image; all appear in the gallery.
4. Build the body in **sections**: each section has a heading, text, and its own optional photos (e.g. Day 1, Day 2, Day 3). Section photos show inline with that section on the notice.
5. Optionally add **video links** (YouTube/Vimeo/Facebook, one per line) and a **programme PDF**.
6. For a Travel & Tourism notice, set the **payment**: none, participation (with a fee), or donation.
7. Publish. Approved members are notified of the new notice.

### Editing a notice
Open a notice's **Edit** screen. You can change any field, add or remove cover
and section photos (tick photos to remove), replace or remove the PDF, and
adjust the payment. Existing sections and their photos are preserved through an
edit.

### Managing members
In **Admin → Members**, approve or reject pending registrations (approval sends
a welcome email), block or unblock accounts, change a member's role, or remove
an account.

### Verifying payments
In **Admin → Donations**, review submitted participation fees and donations.
Verify or reject each one; the member is notified of the outcome. Verified
amounts count toward the tour or fund.

### Polls
In **Admin → Polls**, create a poll with options, then close, reopen, or delete
it. The active poll appears on the Travel board.

### Payment methods
In **Admin → Payments**, configure the bank and mobile payment methods members
see — label, account name, account number, instructions, and whether each is
active.

## The look and feel

The design is deliberately institutional: deep Bangladesh green with national
red accents, a refined "field-log" typographic style, and a **rice-ear crest**
as the emblem — a nod to the food service's remit and to the sheaf on
Bangladesh's national emblem. The result reads as an official, trustworthy
departmental portal rather than a generic app.

## One-line summary

A trusted, members-only home where food officers and employees keep in touch, plan tours,
support one another, and stay one community across every posting.
